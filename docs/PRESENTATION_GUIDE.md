# Verdant Capital pitch deck

[Download PowerPoint](Verdant_Capital_Pitch_Deck.pptx) · [Static PDF](Verdant_Capital_Pitch_Deck.pdf)

1. Mission: Pillar 2 and evidence-led capital allocation.
2. Problem: evidence gaps, rare events and unequal error costs.
3. Solution: ESG verifier, market analyst and constrained optimizer.
4. Product walkthrough: embedded 62-second silent video.
5. C02 and C06: rare-event evaluation and three explained edge cases.
6. C13: explicit cost matrix, selected threshold and test confusion matrix.
7. Thank you and next validation steps.

## Play the video

Download the PPTX and open it in desktop Microsoft PowerPoint. Start Slide Show,
advance to slide 4 and click the video. The MP4 is embedded; Internet is not required.
Web viewers may not support playback. If needed, play the
[standalone MP4](presentation_assets/product_demo.mp4). The PDF contains a static
poster and cannot play video. The supplied recording has no audio and uses the
earlier mint theme; the presentation uses charcoal and gold.

Speaker notes explain the calculations and limitations. C02/C06/C13 are synthetic
prototype evaluations, not verified real-world financial outcomes or ESG accuracy.
